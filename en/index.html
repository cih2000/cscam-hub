01 / COMPANY OVERVIEW

About CSCAM

Under the slogan "Customer Impression," CSCAM promises its customers the best possible service.

Founded in 2000 under Korea's Venture Business Act, CSCAM has continuously invested in R&D — commercializing Korea's first domestic commercial CAD/CAM system, developing dedicated CAD/CAM systems and multi-axis machine tools for tire mold design and manufacturing, and building CAM systems and marking machines for shipbuilding that contributed significantly to design and process automation at Hyundai Heavy Industries, Samsung Heavy Industries, and Hyundai Samho Heavy Industries. Building on years of CNC control technology experience, CSCAM acquired Turbotech's CNC division in 2009 and has since become Korea's leading CNC specialist, developing domestic CNC controllers and servo packs.

Rather than resting on past technology, CSCAM continues developing the core and challenging technologies industry and the field require — commercializing dedicated CAD/CAM systems for mold design and machining, high-performance EtherCAT-based CNC controllers, high-resolution label marking machines, 5-axis On-The-Fly laser patterning machines, metal 3D printers (PBF method), and LMS systems for process automation.

CSCAM is accelerating technology development and commercialization with a vision of technological globalization — responding flexibly to rapid social change and contributing to national technological self-sufficiency.

CEO Greeting

"The future belongs to those who dare." Every member of CSCAM approaches our work with fresh, creative thinking rather than settling for what we already have.

Starting with Korea's leading CAD/CAM system development, our company has spent years building the core technologies the field needs to boost productivity — dedicated CAM systems for tires, shipbuilding automation programs, pre-processing character marking systems, multi-axis machines, wheel-forming systems, CNC technology, and CS_DNC/monitoring systems.
